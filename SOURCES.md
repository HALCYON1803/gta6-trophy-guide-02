# Sources — gta6-trophy-guide-02 (Leonida Ledger)

Every fact in this guide rests on one of the sources below. Trophy names,
descriptions and grades are PlayStation's own words as published; the guides
are this entry's own words, written from these sources and cited per trophy
in `app/data/trophies.json` (each trophy carries its own `sources` list, and
most carry numbered steps of its own).

## The official list (names, descriptions, grades, hidden flags)

- **Exophase / PSNProfiles / TrueAchievements, via PlanetGTA6** —
  https://www.planetgta6.com/en/gta-6-trophies/
  "Reported Official English names and descriptions, as shown on PlayStation
  Network, taken on November 18 from Exophase and cross-checked with
  PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements." This is the source for the 27 visible /
  28 hidden split, every name and grade, and every Xbox Gamerscore value
  (which sums to 1,000G). The kit's own dossier agrees: 55 trophies, 1
  Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze, 28 hidden.

## Guide steps, counts, difficulty, time, missables

- **PowerPyx + GTABase, via GTA-Zone** —
  https://gta-zone.com/news/gta-6-trophies-achievements/
  "The same 55 names and descriptions appear in PowerPyx's trophy guide,
  GTABase, Insider Gaming and PC Gamer. Difficulty, time and missables come
  from PowerPyx, Gamerscore values from GTABase." This is the source for:
  the 4/10 difficulty and ~200-hour platinum estimate (story 60–80h, 100%
  about 100h, cleanup about 10h); the one possibly missable trophy
  (Positive Mental Attitude, Chapter 1 meditation spot); the counts
  (397 stunts / 300 for 100%, 42 Shooting Range challenges across 12 ranges,
  14 drone-race locations / 42 races, 20 of 40 races, 18 meditation spots in
  10 locations, 3 of 18 card sets, 100 WAiNK locations split 31 Landmarks /
  35 Curiosities / 23 Sculptures / No Flusher tags, 50 of 80 challenges,
  5 of 8 bank vaults, 9 gangs / 39 operations, 200 of 277 vehicles,
  100 of 255 animals, 50 of 66 weapons, 8 garages for sale ~$160,000);
  the Strangers & Freaks counting rules (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete count;
  Moses, Tyrone, Yasmani, Andy, Billy do not); and the per-trophy methods
  (Cal's fishing text, Yasmani's Snap Gun, Mile Zero tank, VCIA jet, Kingpin
  Bowling robbery, Strength for both characters, 500 Criminal Profile
  actions, Scooter Bros app from Moses in Chapter 2). The five trophies it
  lists as "unconfirmed" (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck,
  I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) are marked unconfirmed here too.
- **GTABase trophy guide** —
  https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-achievements-trophies-list-ps5-xbox-series
  Full list with grades, Gamerscore and hidden flags; cross-check for the
  counts and the roadmap above.
- **OutputLag trophy list** —
  https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-list/
  Cross-check on the 55 / 1-2-6-46 split, the 28 hidden trophies, the
  Chapter 1 meditation missable, no mission replay, ~200 hours, no online
  trophies, and cheats disabling trophies.

## What is new in this entry versus gta6-trophy-guide-01

- A **Next Up planner** that ranks the unearned non-story trophies by
  difficulty, then estimated hours, then roadmap phase, with a plain-words
  reason for each pick.
- **Six roadmap phases** (was five), a **chapter progress** view (Chapters
  1–5), a **dashboard** (progress ring, Gamerscore, hours left, missable
  and unconfirmed counts, per-grade bars), **sorting** (roadmap / easiest /
  name / grade / chapter), an **unconfirmed-only filter**, **personal
  notes per trophy** (saved in the browser), and a **copy progress summary**
  button.
- Every trophy now carries **numbered steps** as well as its guide paragraph,
  its **roadmap phase**, and its own **time estimate**; the guides were
  rewritten in this entry's own words.
- A new dark "Vice City nights" look, a new file layout (`js/store.js`,
  `js/filters.js`, `js/views.js`, `js/boot.js`, `css/theme.css`).

## What is NOT a source here

- No Rockstar Games logo, official artwork, box art or game screenshot is
  used anywhere in this entry.
- No guide text is copied from any of the sources above: each guide is
  written in this entry's own words, and the source is cited beside it.
- Where the sources disagree or say "unconfirmed", this guide says so
  rather than picking a side silently. The game launched 19 November 2026;
  details may change as players crack the last five triggers.
