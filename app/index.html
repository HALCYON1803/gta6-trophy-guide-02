About this guide

This guide is an independent, fan-made tracker. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Sony or PlayStation, and it uses no Rockstar logo, artwork or game screenshots: the look is its own.

How accurate is it?

The trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official list as PlayStation publishes it, mirrored by Exophase and PSNProfiles on 18 November 2026 and cross-checked against TrueAchievements, GTABase and PowerPyx. The guides are written in this entry's own words from those sources, with numbered steps and a source link beside every trophy. Where a trophy's exact trigger has not been pinned down yet — five trophies, marked unconfirmed — the guide says so instead of inventing steps. Difficulty ratings and time estimates are the guide's own estimates, labelled as such; the headline figures (about 200 hours, 4/10) are PowerPyx's, via GTA-Zone.

How to use it

Tick a trophy off when you earn it on PS5; the tick is saved in this browser's localStorage and survives reloads. Use Export to keep a backup file, and Import to restore it on another device. Spoilers stay off by default, so the 28 hidden trophies show as “Hidden trophy” until you earn them or turn spoilers on. Add a personal note to any trophy — it is saved with your progress. The whole guide is static files that work with the network off: nothing is fetched at run time except its own trophy data file. Press / to jump to search.

Key facts, with sources

  • 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze; 28 hidden; 1,000 Gamerscore on Xbox. (PlanetGTA6, GTABase)
  • No online trophies and no DLC trophies; the whole list is single-player. (GTA-Zone, GTABase)
  • Platinum about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10; story 60–80 hours. (PowerPyx via GTA-Zone)
  • One possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude, first meditation spot in Chapter 1. (PowerPyx via GTA-Zone)
  • Five trophies unconfirmed: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin. (GTA-Zone)
  • Cheats disable trophies and saving, and only unlock after the story. (PowerPyx via GTA-Zone)
  • No mission replay and no New Game+; free roam continues after the story. (GTA-Zone)
